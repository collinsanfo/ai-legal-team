# Jurisdiction research checklist

Give researcher A the A topics and researcher B the B topics, for every country in scope. Skip topics the
chosen documents do not need (see `checklists/document-scoping.md`) and add product-specific questions.
Every answer needs a primary source and a confidence level.

## Set A: people, data, contracts, content
1. **Data protection:** registration with the regulator (who, when, fees, renewal, offence if missing);
   the mandatory privacy-notice contents; lawful bases and consent; sensitive data (health, children,
   biometrics) and its conditions; data subject rights and response times; processor contracts; security
   and breach notification (to whom, how fast); transfers abroad; retention rules and record-keeping
   periods; data protection officer; children's consent age; direct marketing consent.
2. **Electronic contracts:** validity of click-wrap and browse-wrap; terms readable before agreeing;
   correcting input errors; keeping records.
3. **Consumer protection online:** supplier information that must be shown; cooling-off or no-reason
   cancellation rights (periods, exemptions such as services already started, perishables, custom goods,
   dated accommodation or transport); performance deadlines; refund timelines; what cannot be contracted
   out.
4. **Contracts and liability:** exclusion and limitation clauses; caps; unfair terms; changing terms by
   notice; third parties relying on the platform's terms.
5. **Capacity:** age of majority; contracts with minors.
6. **Content:** copyright licence for user posts; defamation, privacy and image rights; online offences;
   intermediary safe harbour and the notice-and-take-down process; duties to report illegal content.
7. **Disputes:** arbitration against consumers; mediation; courts and small claims.
8. **Company disclosure:** what an online business must show about itself.
9. **Cookies and tracking:** consent for cookies, SDKs and pixels; what counts as strictly necessary.
10. **Platform duties:** notice and action, reasons for moderation decisions, appeals, trader checks,
    ranking transparency, transparency reports.
11. **Children and online safety:** age limits, parental consent, design codes, risk assessments.
12. **AI:** transparency and risk rules for AI features; automated decisions about people.
13. **Accessibility:** whether accessibility laws apply to the product.
14. **Reviews and advertising:** fake reviews, endorsements, sponsored content, ad labels.
15. **Platform policies that act like law:** app-store rules (privacy answers, account deletion,
    subscriptions) and the payment processor's terms.

## Set B: money, tax, sectors
1. **Payments licensing:** does collecting buyers' money and paying sellers later need a licence? What
   about a processor's split payments? Any exemption for a commercial agent?
2. **The payment processor's own terms:** marketplace rules, splits, settlement timing, who bears refunds
   and chargebacks, prohibited businesses.
3. **Anti-money laundering:** is the platform an accountable or obliged entity?
4. **Tax:** VAT or sales tax on the platform's fee; registration thresholds; withholding; reporting
   sellers' income; record-keeping periods.
5. **Sector rules**, for each live category: who must hold what licence (the seller or the platform), what
   a listing must show, and any platform duty. Typical categories: accommodation and student housing
   (rent-advance caps, tourism licensing); medical aesthetics and health services (practitioner and
   facility licensing); cosmetics, supplements and medical devices (product registration; whether
   "selling" includes displaying); food and catering (permits); vehicle sale and rental; courier and
   moving; pet care and veterinary work; massage and fitness; events; recruitment (employment-agency
   licensing, non-discrimination).
6. **Prohibited and restricted goods:** narcotics, weapons, wildlife, counterfeit and stolen goods,
   tobacco and alcohol rules, prescription medicines, precious metals, chemicals; plus a major
   marketplace's restricted-products list as a baseline.
7. **Subscriptions:** automatic renewal, free trials, cancellation and price-change rules.
8. **Physical goods:** product safety and labelling, legal guarantees, returns.
9. **Promotions:** contests and giveaways, gift cards and vouchers, loyalty schemes.
10. **Business customers:** data processing terms, transfer clauses, liability between businesses.

## Worked example: Ghana (from one run, September 2026; verify current status before relying on it)
| Area | Laws the run relied on |
|---|---|
| Data protection | Data Protection Act 2012 (Act 843): registration (s.53, s.56), notice contents (s.27), special personal data (s.37), processors (s.30), breaches (s.31) |
| Electronic transactions and consumers | Electronic Transactions Act 2008 (Act 772): terms readable before agreeing (s.17(2)), e-contracts (s.23), supplier information (s.47), cooling-off (s.49), no contracting out (s.54), hosting safe harbour (s.92), take-down notices (s.94) |
| Cybersecurity | Cybersecurity Act 2020 (Act 1038): incident reporting, retention (s.77, applicability to verify) |
| Payments | Payment Systems and Services Act 2019 (Act 987): licence (s.7), offence (s.9) |
| Anti-money laundering | Anti-Money Laundering Act 2020 (Act 1044) |
| Tax | Value Added Tax Act 2025 (Act 1151); Revenue Administration Act 2016 (Act 915), records kept in Ghana (s.27) |
| Contracts and goods | Contracts Act 1960 (Act 25); Sale of Goods Act 1962 (Act 137) |
| Capacity | Children's Act 1998 (Act 560): a child is under 18 |
| Disputes | Alternative Dispute Resolution Act 2010 (Act 798) |
| Companies | Companies Act 2019 (Act 992); Registration of Business Names Act 1962 (Act 151) |
| Accommodation | Rent Act 1963 (Act 220) s.25(5) advance-rent cap, s.26 agents; Ghana Tourism Act 2011 (Act 817) |
| Health and products | Public Health Act 2012 (Act 851): FDA registration, "sell" includes display (s.149), no online tobacco sales (s.68(3)); Health Professions Regulatory Bodies Act 2013 (Act 857); Health Institutions and Facilities Act 2011 (Act 829) |
| Vehicles, courier | Road Traffic Act 2004 (Act 683) and L.I. 2180; Postal and Courier Services Regulatory Commission Act 2003 (Act 649) |
| Content | Copyright Act 2005 (Act 690) |
| Employment | Labour Act 2003 (Act 651) |
| Banned goods | Narcotics Control Commission Act 2020 (Act 1019); Wildlife Resources Management Act 2023 (Act 1115); gold trading licensing (Act 1140) |
