# The document set and what each must contain

Adapt the list to the product. For each document, check the jurisdiction's mandatory contents first
(see `checklists/jurisdiction-research.md`).

## Public documents

### Terms of Service (everyone)
Who the operator is (legal name, registration number, address, phone, email); how users accept, versions
and changes; what the product is and is not (not the seller, not an employer, not a bank, and so on);
defined terms; eligibility and age; accounts; using the product as a buyer (bookings, requests, orders,
address privacy); payments in short; statutory rights that cannot be removed; user content and the
licence granted; reviews and posts; AI features in short; acceptable use; reporting and the take-down
contact; the operator's role and a reasonable liability cap with lawful carve-outs; suspension and account
closure (truthful); disputes (no forced arbitration where the law protects consumers); general clauses;
contact. Category terms as separate sections where risks differ.

### Privacy Policy (everyone)
The jurisdiction's mandatory notice items; who the controller is and its registration number with the
data protection authority; what is collected by category, why, and the legal basis; sensitive data (or
that none is collected); who sees what (sellers, staff, the public); every outside service by name and
type, what it receives and where it is; transfers abroad; retention per record type, backups; rights and
how to use them, with timelines; marketing consent and opt-out; minimum age; AI in short; location; push
and email; browser storage; security without overclaiming; breach notification; changes and versions;
contact and how to complain to the regulator.

### Business (seller) Terms
Who can sell; accepting the terms; the seller is the seller; accurate listings, licences and permits
(self-declared is never shown as verified); the prohibited-listings policy; prices, the platform fee and
payouts under the real payment model; refunds and chargebacks; taxes; buyer data rules; reviews and posts
about the seller; insider and self-dealing rules; suspension; ending the agreement; liability and
indemnity (reasonable); disputes; category schedules.

### Payments and Refunds policy (buyers and sellers)
How paying works under the real model; ways to pay that really exist; what buyers pay; cancellations and
the statutory cooling-off right; declined or expired requests; no-shows (without the platform deciding who
is right); automatic completion; tips; products and returns; where refunds go (the original method);
payouts; chargebacks; requests that take no payment.

### Community Guidelines
What may and may not be posted; reviews; messages; live content; how to report, what happens next, who
reviews and how fast (truthfully); enforcement; the take-down contact; content about children and
vulnerable people.

### Prohibited and restricted listings
Banned and restricted goods and services, each with its legal basis and confidence; how listings are
checked (truthfully); self-declaration; the catch-all "other" category; what happens on a breach.

### AI Features Notice
What each AI feature is; what it can see; what is sent to which provider and whether it is stored; limits
(can be wrong, no professional advice, cannot decide or pay); what not to share; how to stop using it.

### Consent texts (where needed)
Short, versioned, one tick per thing agreed; no more data than needed.

## Internal documents
- **Data map and retention:** a record of processing (data, where stored, purpose, legal basis, who can
  access, outside services, retention, deletion behaviour); processor register; retention schedule with
  legal bases; regulator registration checklist; breach and data-request procedures; high-risk review;
  code changes needed.
- **Questions for counsel**, **App changes needed**, **Law research**: built by `scripts/build_pack.py`.
- **Decider's answers** (optional) and **Red-team review**.
