# Owner question bank

Ask only what changes the documents. One question each, recommendation first. Record answers in section A
of the owner-answers-and-law-digest file, with the date.

| # | Topic | Question | Usual recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scope | How do the Terms describe the product: in general terms, by naming each offering, or both? | Both: a general description plus a clear list, and a short list of what the operator is not |
| 2 | Launch scope | Which categories open at launch? | Only those whose conditions (licences, checks) are ready |
| 3 | Category terms | Which categories need their own terms (stays, vehicle hire, food, regulated services)? | Each category with its own risks gets its own section |
| 4 | Words | What do the documents call the people who sell: business, provider, seller, professional, practitioner? | One umbrella word, defined once, plus trade names where a rule is category-specific |
| 5 | Operator | Who runs the product: exact legal name, registration number, address, phone, email? | A registered company as the contracting party |
| 6 | Age | What is the minimum age? | The age of majority where users live |
| 7 | Dates and versions | Effective date, last-updated date, or both? Separate versions per document? | Both dates; a version per document |
| 8 | Changes | What counts as a change, and must users accept again? | Every change released as a new version that users accept; keep existing rights for people who have not |
| 9 | Sign-up consent | Tick box, or a "by continuing you agree" line, for email and social sign-in? | Terms readable before agreeing; record who accepted which version, when and how |
| 10 | Payments model | Does the operator collect money and pay sellers out, or does a payment processor split it? | Whatever needs no payments licence; check the law first |
| 11 | Fees | Who pays the platform fee, and how much? Who pays the processor fee? | Sellers pay the platform fee; processor fee split in proportion |
| 12 | Tips | Are tips refundable? | Refund a tip when the service never happens |
| 13 | Cancellations and returns | What refund rules apply, and how do they fit the statutory cooling-off rights? | Never below the statutory minimum |
| 14 | Deletion and retention | What is kept after an account is deleted, and for how long? Backups? | The legal period per record type, then permanent deletion |
| 15 | Outside services | Name each processor, or describe them by type? | Both, and tell users at the point of use |
| 16 | Sensitive data | Does the product need health, children's or identity data at all? | Do not collect it unless the product cannot work without it |
| 17 | Take-down contact | Where do content and copyright complaints go? | A published address and email, checked by a person |
| 18 | Seller details | Which seller details do buyers see? | Whatever the law requires sellers to disclose, before purchase and on receipts |
| 19 | Banned and restricted goods | What basis for the prohibited list? | Local law plus a major marketplace's restricted list |
| 20 | Vulnerable people | Which services involving children or vulnerable adults are allowed? | Ban sole care; allow services with a parent or carer present |
| 21 | AI features | What may the AI see and say; is anything stored? | Disclose the provider; no advice; no decisions about people |
