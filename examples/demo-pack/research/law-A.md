# Memo A: people, data, contracts and content (fictional example)

All laws in this memo are fictional. They show the memo format only.

## Summary
1. Operators that process personal data must register with the Data Protection Office before
   processing (Data Protection Act 2019 (fictional), s.10). Confidence: high.
2. A privacy notice must name the controller, the purposes, the recipients and the rights (s.18).
   Confidence: high.
3. Consumers can cancel distance contracts for services within 7 days unless the service has started
   with their agreement (Consumer Protection Act 2015 (fictional), s.30). Confidence: medium.

## 1. Registration
- **Answer:** Register before processing; renew every two years.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Sources:** Data Protection Act 2019 (fictional), s.10, https://example.org/data-protection-act
- **Means for Tidyhome:** register before launch and show the number in the Privacy Policy.
- **Ask counsel:** whether a pre-launch test counts as processing.
